# Traveler Profile & Trip-Planning Guide

## Pacing & Daily Structure
I like to have 3-5 primary activities or destinations in a full day. I like to start off my mornings (9am - 12pm) with breakfast, then a major cultural site, walking tour, or outdoor excursion. In the afternoon, I like to have lunch, then check out a museum, shop, or take a nap, especially in the heat. In the evening, I love catching a sunset, having a relaxed dinner, and winding down with a neighborhood walk. At night, I like time to journal, process photos, and plan the next day. I like not being rushed at an attraction or place. I also like traveling at a sustainable pace: A 7-mile walking day should be followed by a lot of sleep and a chill day.

## Likes:
 * Nature & Waterways: Gardens, parks, lakes, coastal paths, scenic lookouts, and volcanic or geothermal landscapes.
* Architecture & Design: Historic blocks, distinct regional architecture, public transit infrastructure, and niche museums (e.g., glass-making, textiles, local history).
* Rituals & Wellness: Daily fresh fruit/juices/coffee, local bakeries, spots to sit and journal, and massages.    
* Dietary restriction: accessible vegetarian-friendly options

## Dislikes:
* Night Markets and clubbing:  just get overwhelmed by the sheer number of stuff there is to buy and the large, slowly moving mass of people. I typically don't buy anything from these markets, and I don't find many food options that work for me.  I do enjoy cocktail lounges 
* Crowded Tourist Traps: Over-hyped, shoulder-to-shoulder experiences (e.g., hyper-crowded viewpoints, narrow stair-climbs like Diamond Head, or excessively congested sights).
* Too many art museums in a row become boring. Too many gardens also become boring. Having a variety of experiences is fulfilling.
