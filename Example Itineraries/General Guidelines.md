### What do I look for in making a trip for myself?  
Interesting to me personally:   
- Architecture (Gaudi houses, Osaka Castle), especially when there are dioramas   
- Gardens and parks (Japan, Portland Oregon).  
- Waterways and beaches (Tahoe, Kawaguchiko)   
- Seasonal events (cherry blossoms, northern lights)  
- Walkability/transit (Florence, Tokyo)  
- Geology (Big Island of Hawaii)  
- Interesting museums (National Museum of Ethnology Osaka, Ad Museum Tokyo)  
- Geospatial machine learning to tackle climate resiliency challenges. Also climate resiliency in general   
- People watching   
  
My personal hobbies in order of how much time I spend with them:  
- Digital Journaling   
- Books. Reading on the beach is nice. Reading is nice.   
- Playing Video games   
- Hiking (but nothing intense, like less than 6 miles round trip and less than 500ft elevation). overcrowded hikes aren’t fun. Hikes to lookout points aren’t fulfilling. So I suppose I’m picky with my hikes. In general I find the effort to value ratio low, so maybe I don’t want to do hikes at all   
- Biking (but nothing intense, like an hour at a time to interesting places)   
- Frisbee   
- Observational Drawing  
- Stargazing (Tasmania)  
  
I value  
- Personal safety and safety of belongings   
- Walkability  
- Being connected to the Internet    
- Variety of experiences   
- Being comfortable with the vegetarian options available so I can eat well.   
- Traveling at a sustainable pace. A 7 mile walking day should be followed by a lot of sleep and a chill day. Not being rushed at an attraction or place. Not moving between hotels too much. Being able to take breaks during the day or in between days because walking is exhausting. Having time in the evenings to write in my journal or organize photos.   
- Comfortable flights. Although I pick the cheapest flight available, I try not to have red eye flights, and prefer nonstop flights to ones with layovers. I prefer my layovers to be 3-5 hours long.   
  
I don’t care for:  
- clubbing or spending a night drinking, though I do enjoy the occasional cocktail.   
- Different food experiences, my main priority when traveling is making sure I can eat enough to have energy and get my nutrients.   
- Too many art museums becomes boring (Italy). Too many gardens also becomes boring (Japan). That’s why I said having a variety of experiences is fulfilling.   
- Night markets. I think the idea is cool. I just get overwhelmed by the sheer number of stuff there is to buy and the large slowly moving mass of people. I typically don’t buy anything from these markets. I don’t care about acquiring souvenirs or cheap clothes. And sometimes these markets have food - but it’s hard to find vegetarian food. A lot of the food is fried - I’d rather not eat fried food as that tends to mess up my stomach.   
  
I also think I prefer traveling with a partner than by myself. In Australia and Spain, I spent about 10 days each alone, and both times, halfway through, I felt very depressed. It’s hard to determine if that was due to exhaustion (I was traveling at an unsustainable pace) or loneliness. I do believe sharing a travel experience, such as a museum or a view or food, is more fulfilling than writing about it in my journal. I’ve thought about staying at hostels, but am uncomfortable with having to share a bedroom with multiple people.
