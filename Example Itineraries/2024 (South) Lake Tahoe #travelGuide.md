**HIKES**  
**Easy**  
* ++[Cascade Falls Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/ar/trail/us/california/cascade-falls--7)++  
    * **Difficulty:** Easy | 0.5 miles out and back | 134 ft elevation gain  
    * **Time:** 15 min  
    * **Route:** Nice and short hike to a waterfall surrounded by towering redwoods.  
**derate**  
* ++[Glen Alpine Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/ar/trail/us/california/glen-alpine-trail)++  
    * **Difficulty:** Moderate | 6.8 miles out and back | 1,371 ft elevation gain  
    * **Time:** 3 hr 23 min  
    * **Route:** Starts near Lily Lake and passes some waterfalls within the first half mile of the trail. The trail heads uphill and goes up a series of switchbacks. There are great views of the surrounding mountains as you make your way up.   
* ******++[Fallen Leaf Lake Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/ar/trail/us/california/fallen-leaf-lake-trail--4)++   
    * **Difficulty:** Moderate | 3.5 miles out and back | 144 ft elevation gain  
    * **Time:** 2 hr  
**Hard**   
* ++[Granite Lake & Maggie’s Peaks](https://www.alltrails.com/ar/trail/us/california/granite-lake-maggies-peaks)++   
    * **Difficulty:** Hard | 4.1 miles out and back | 1,771 ft elevation gain  
    * **Time:** 3-4 hours roundtrip due to the steepness of the route  
    * **Cost:** Free to park on the road, $10 to park in the parking lot
