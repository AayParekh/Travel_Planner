# 2025 Hawaii #travelGuide  
  
## Big Island/Island of Hawaii   
Hotel: [Wild Ginger Hotel](https://maps.app.goo.gl/aiu2DZUZ9bk7yvdy5). Breakfast included. Rooms don’t have AC but they do have two fans. Bug nets aren’t very secure.    
##   
### Monday May 19  
- Land at Kona Airport  
- Rent a car, drive across the island to Hilo (90 minutes in the dark and rain without wifi, don’t recommend it)   
- Check into [Howzit Hostels](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tTXuTy9H8ZYeXx8A9), decide it’s not for me, find and move to Wild Ginger Hotel.   
  
### Tuesday May 20  
- Lunch at [Pineapple's Island Fresh Cuisine](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XP3vVqn9PqsmB55QA)  
- [Hawai’i tropical botanical gardens](https://maps.app.goo.gl/MW5gL9QjHusdDAJJ8). Spent 2-3 hours here.   
- Dinner at [Kamana Kitchen](https://maps.app.goo.gl/BfVAwXgjJ17YfF8y6)  
- Sunset at [Richardson Ocean Park](https://maps.app.goo.gl/mmeLNmUc1QPdUPk69)  
  
### Wednesday May 21  
- Breakfast at [Ken’s House of Pancakes](https://maps.app.goo.gl/dE58DGs1gpDHnn2u7)  
- [Tour](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionProductReview-g60583-d11447167-Volcano_Adventure_Hilo_Sightseeing_w_Optional_Helicopter_Tour-Hilo_Island_of_Hawaii.html)  
    - [Lili'uokalani Gardens](https://maps.app.goo.gl/vxKFFEFhQ4aSoPEi6). <30 minutes.   
    - [Rainbow Falls](https://maps.app.goo.gl/PMNz3YxAqfGWgs1k9). <30 minutes.   
    - Hawaii Volcanoes National Park (Kilauea Volcano, Steam Vents)  
        - [Nahuku - Thurston Lava Tube](https://maps.app.goo.gl/bhsozUHxMP1Dtym87). <1 hour  
        - [Luamanu Crater](https://maps.app.goo.gl/gdxJq4YS1GpnEVYt7) and lava field. <30 minutes  
    - [Akaka Falls State Park](https://maps.app.goo.gl/iXK5vJRkMsPaitEN9)  
- Dinner at [Pineapple's Island Fresh Cuisine](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XP3vVqn9PqsmB55QA)

### Thursday May 22  
- Quick visit to [Hilo Farmer's Market](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Pi4k5MtLGELHcWuV6). It was half-open.   
- [Laupahoehoe Train Museum](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ydYVBtxi8TtgNkfM7). <1 hour  
- [Laupāhoehoe Point](https://maps.app.goo.gl/YxrGc9hwfiKKF9cE7). <1 hour

- [Waipio Fruitshack](https://maps.app.goo.gl/m3uQ9g72BbExSfgj8). There were many such fruit trucks/stands on the road, I picked randomly. It was cute and good though.   
- [Waipio Valley Lookout](https://maps.app.goo.gl/2pA3e3hgM7B9DEZX9). <30 minutes.   
- [Kua Bay beach](https://maps.app.goo.gl/SBTbYtqw5fCnegLP6)  
- Flight from Kona to Oahu  
- Dinner at Zippy’s. Don’t recommend.   
  
Didn’t have time in Oahu for Mauna Kea Stargazing  
  
## Oahu  
[AirBnB](https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/27910397?source_impression_id=p3_1751229148_P3kfJhbH91wqBCTS). Very convenient location.   
  
### Friday May 23  
- Brunch at [Goofy Cafe](https://maps.app.goo.gl/YWFa7sxXjY5oq3RH9)  
- Walk around AirBnB, Kahanamoku Beach, Waikiki Beach, and downtown Waikiki (Waikiki Beach Walk, International Market Place)  
- Lunch at [Noi Thai](https://maps.app.goo.gl/zZQK73fhvnQewJPBA)  
- Beach time at [Kahanamoku Beach](https://maps.app.goo.gl/JxP7s8fCeT9thwXU9)  
- Fireworks at 7:45pm (5 minutes)  
- Dinner at [Monkeypod Kitchen](https://maps.app.goo.gl/gSqfQbArwzXGer9s5). Be prepared to wait >1 hour for a table. Food is great though.   
  
### Saturday May 24  
- Breakfast at [Guieb Cafe](https://maps.app.goo.gl/zQp3Lk7dH7m2cPDx7). Known for ube pancakes  
- [Dole Plantation](https://maps.app.goo.gl/4fZiMzuTQ6Bhn3qDA)  
    - Train ride around the plantation. Be prepared to wait <1 hour. Train ride is ~30 minutes long   
    - Dole whip   
    - Maze. 1-2 hours   
    - Gardens. Skippable.   
- [Waimea Falls](https://maps.app.goo.gl/kHZCi72ZSb9LDg2F6). They say it’s a moderate hike but I’d say it’s easy - very little elevation, very dry when we went, and took ~30-45 minutes one way. Need to wear a life jacket if you want to go into the lake at the base of the falls.   
- Lunch at [Killer Tacos](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Z57M8WSmiactL6yt5)  
- Sunset at [Sunset Beach](https://maps.app.goo.gl/okGhJccby1DC8dts6)  
- Dinner at [Fooki](https://maps.app.goo.gl/d1SsPcWuWjyQx5xr6)  
  
### Sunday May 25  
- Breakfast at [Goofy Cafe](https://maps.app.goo.gl/YWFa7sxXjY5oq3RH9). Be prepared for a >1 hour wait.   
- [Pearl Harbor Museum](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yYeT37isXQqpZ1vYA). Went to the [USS Arizona Memorial](https://maps.app.goo.gl/3wb4ZJ6htrZc15ca6) as well. Total <3 hours. Didn’t go to the aviation museum.   
- Lunch at [Earl’s Sandwiches](https://maps.app.goo.gl/q298Wrr7X2EteRd96)  
- ++[Halona Blowhole](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/hawaii/oahu/halona-blowhole--2)++. You can walk down to it.   
- ++[Makapuʻu Point se Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/hawaii/oahu/makapu-u-point-lighthouse-trail).++ I consider it an easy trail, took ~1 hour to get to the lighthouse and there is a tough uphill section.   
- Dinner at [Camado Ramen Tavern](https://maps.app.goo.gl/WDiuJoobKkSVmDsAA)   
  
### Monday May 26  
- Breakfast at [Honolulu Coffee at Moana Surfrider](https://maps.app.goo.gl/h7UKh6msVy7Y3pdY9)?   
- [Diamond Head](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/hawaii/oahu/diamond-head-leahi-summit-trail) hike. This was a difficult hike, it’s just stairs for 30 minutes with no shade. The view up there is nice but it’s too crowded to enjoy it.   
- [Kailua Beach Adventures kayaking](https://www.kailuabeachadventures.com). We did the easiest/most popular route, which is 0.5 miles out to [Mokulua Islets Seabird Sanctuary](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Qc4KWqjFTnNnRR1z8), then 1 mile back. Lunch is provided after the trip.   
- Shaved ice from [Island Snow](https://maps.app.goo.gl/CBLk2L5uVsSyhVZW8)  
- Dinner at [Monkeypod Kitchen](https://maps.app.goo.gl/gSqfQbArwzXGer9s5).   
  
### Tuesday May 27

- Lunch at [Brug Bakery Lanai](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tWwWwAJU1s8wt3qA6) and boba from [85C](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5Dw5uHWUdji7EK9d6)  
- Dinner at [Kamana Kitchen](https://maps.app.goo.gl/6sKGYCiU2NpCGCza7)??
