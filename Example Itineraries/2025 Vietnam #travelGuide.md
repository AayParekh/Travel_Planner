## Pretravel info  
- Rough conversion: 100,000 VND ~ 4 USD  
- Sign up for a Visa [here](https://evisa.gov.vn/e-visa/foreigners). It took 3 days for mine to get approved.   
- Be sure to pack sunscreen, bug spray, itch cream, Imodium, and a hat

## Retrospective   
### Keep  
- eating fresh fruit and drinking fresh fruit juice (coconuts are amazing)  
- Calling taxis/bikes to get around quickly and avoiding walking too much in the heat   
- Getting massages   
- Drinking at least 3L a day   
- Having 1,000,000 VND on hand every day to pay for food and other things  
- Using Schwab debit card for no transaction fees at ATMs. Helps in a pinch and can get exactly what you need instead of overestimating   
  
### Change  
- Do not get lemonade or other drinks that involve water from random stands or even restaurants. There’s no guarantee they boil the water. I got lemonade from a stand and was sick for 10 days. Stick to pure fruit juice like coconut or pineapple or watermelon   
  
### Start  
- Have a plan for rain. A list of Indoor places like massages or museums, or coffee shops to pull from when it rains will be helpful
