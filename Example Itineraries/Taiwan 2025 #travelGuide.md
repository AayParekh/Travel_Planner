## Pretravel info  
- Rough conversion: 100 TWD ~ 3 USD  
- Sign up for an arrival card [here](https://twac.immigration.gov.tw).   
- Be sure to pack sunscreen, bug spray, itch cream, Imodium, and a hat  
- Plenty of family marts and 7-11s to buy snacks and drinks at any time or to get cash   
  
Hotel: [Royal Seasons Hotel Taipei](https://maps.google.com?q=Royal%20Seasons%20Hotel%20Taipei,%20330%E8%99%9F,%20Nanjing%20W%20Rd,%20Datong%20District,%20Taipei%20City,%20Taiwan%20103&ftid=0x0:0x8706dd70b537ca38&entry=gps&lucs=,94259549,94275306,94224825,94227247,94227248,94231188,47071704,47069508,94218641,94203019,47084304&g_st=com.google.maps.preview.copy)
